# Later Public-Version Design

Status: **DEFERRED**

Do not load this brief for private-prototype work unless the active change affects future extensibility.

## Initial public target

Level-30 Mining/Forging/Combat content, Bronze/Iron/Steel tiers, two underground dungeons, one village/mine/shop, daily and weekly leaderboards, and earned activity/dungeon pets. New professions reuse the shared contracts and may be free or permanently paid-exclusive through a generic unlock policy.

## Social and collection

Players share the village and display equipment, pets, and achievements but do not initially trade or cooperate. Future boards use server-validated period-specific scores with personal comparisons and mostly cosmetic/modest-Gold rewards. Pets are rare permanent discoveries with small related buffs; all unlocked types may be visible, with client-side visibility/performance controls.

## Monetization

Design the supporter pass only after the prototype. Purchases must be calm, explicit, restorable, and understandable to a child. No fake scarcity, failure nags, paid random loot/pets, or paid luck in the accepted direction. A later direct Robux loot pet may provide permanent auto-banking equivalent to the losable earned Auto-Loot Ring.

## Art and content

Retain stylized low-poly heroic fantasy and a cohesive licensed asset family. Prioritize a few identity-defining hero assets after validation. Final name/lore, full-body armour, Ranged/Magic/Ammo rules, detailed leaderboards, supporter benefits, paid-profession catalog, live operations, trading/guilds/PvP/co-op, and exact later pricing remain deferred decisions.
