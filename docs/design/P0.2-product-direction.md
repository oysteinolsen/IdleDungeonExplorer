# P0.2 — Product Direction

Status: **DONE**

## Product promise

Build a mobile-first Roblox idle/incremental dungeon RPG inspired by Melvor Idle without copying its content, presentation, names, or balance. Players train interconnected professions, prepare gear and supplies, and use that preparation to push increasingly dangerous dungeons. The private prototype proves the complete loop and whether it is fun before public-content production or monetization.

## Core loop

Mining produces ore; Forging turns materials into melee equipment; equipment and supplies enable dungeon progress; dungeon loot can be secured, used, or sold; Gold buys durable progression such as profession tools and bank capacity. Combat, Mining, and Forging are the initial systems. New professions should reuse shared action, inventory, progression, offline, save, analytics, and UI contracts.

## Product invariants

- Dungeon exploration is the aspiration and professions materially determine readiness.
- The first iteration is solo in play but socially visible in a compact shared village. Cooperation and trading are out of scope.
- Active first clears use a small 3D room; repeat and offline runs use the same deterministic combat rules.
- Player progress must be legible, respectful of time, and usable on a small phone.
- Monetization is postponed until the loop works, is non-aggressive, and never relies on paid random loot or paid luck in the accepted initial direction.
- Content is data-defined with stable IDs so expanding professions does not require new save, inventory, offline, or UI frameworks.

## Private-prototype success

The developer can understand and safely change the complete vertical slice, complete it without manual data repair, and still wants another run after repeated development testing. Rough presentation and representative test content are acceptable. Public leaderboards, monetization, and outside testers are not required.
