# P1.4 — Profession Progression

Status: **DONE**

## Level model

- Profession progression is a long-tail journey toward level 99. Level 30 is the first public-content cap, not the end of progression.
- Cumulative XP remains authoritative and continues above the current content cap. Effective level and unlock checks use `min(levelFromTotalXp, configuredContentCap)`.
- Raising a cap applies stored XP immediately. Published curve versions are immutable; replacing one requires a migration preserving level and fractional progress.
- XP awards compose versioned global, profession, and action multipliers separately from earned player modifiers. Test multipliers never come from clients.

`profession_curve.v1` contains explicit integer thresholds for levels 1–99 and reaches 15,000,000 XP. Key checkpoints are level 10: 1,317; 20: 5,093; 30: 15,259; 50: 116,279; 70: 848,252; 80: 2,284,875; 90: 6,151,975; 92: 7,499,542; and 99: 15,000,000.

## Unlock and tool model

- Actions, resources, recipes, tools, and zones declare profession-level requirements.
- Gathering requires an owned tool of the configured category. Gold-bought quality tiers are permanent account unlocks and cannot be sold, consumed, or lost on death.
- Better tools primarily reduce action intervals through the shared modifier pipeline, online and offline.
- New accounts start with 100 Gold; the starter pickaxe costs 50 Gold and must never be blocked behind a no-tool/no-income deadlock.

Representative ladder: Copper/Tin and Bronze Bars at level 1; Bronze weapon at 5; Bronze armour at 8; pickaxe upgrades at 10 and 20; Iron Ore/Bars at 15; Coal and representative Iron equipment at 20; Steel Bars at 25; representative Steel equipment at 30.

Per-action mastery and a shared mastery pool are deferred until the core profession loop has enough actions and playtest evidence. The private prototype awards profession XP only.
