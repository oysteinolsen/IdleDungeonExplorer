# P2.3 — Minimal Village and Mine

Status: **NEXT** — the first visual playtest found blocking collision, seam, HUD, and spatial-interaction issues. Fixes and automated checks are complete; desktop and small-phone re-testing remains.

**Read with:** [`P6.2-device-validation.md`](P6.2-device-validation.md) for the remaining manual check.

- Use a compact above-ground classic-fantasy village. Spawn faces an obvious objective; the mine, dungeon, forge/shop, and future leaderboard locations are only seconds apart.
- The mine and dungeon are separate, visually distinctive entrances. Both lead underground, but productive and dangerous routes must not be confused.
- The first implementation may keep village, mine, and isolated dungeon spaces in one Roblox place while services remain place-independent.
- Art direction is stylized low-poly heroic fantasy with blocky readability, warm village lighting, cooler underground lighting, recognizable Roblox avatars, and mobile-safe geometry/effects.
- The current prototype uses repository-owned primitives, built-in materials, and lights only. No third-party asset or license audit is needed for these fixtures.
- If assets are later introduced, prefer a single cohesive free family, script-free models, verified creators/CC0 sources, and an asset ledger covering source, creator, license, date, local file, Roblox ID, and modifications.
- Village route decorations that are not intended as obstacles must not collide with the avatar. Tunnel, threshold, chamber-floor, wall, and roof pieces deliberately overlap at their seams so a player cannot see or fall through gaps.
- Each visible ore node exposes Roblox's built-in proximity prompt for keyboard and touch input. The server independently requires the avatar to remain within 10 studs of an ore node's surface and stops Mining when the avatar leaves that range.
- The Mining HUD is hidden while idle. During Mining it uses a compact top panel for status/progress and one Stop button, leaving the bottom Roblox tool hotbar unobstructed.

- **Fix build pending re-test:** the brazier is non-colliding; the extended tunnel overlaps entrance/chamber thresholds; chamber-front wall panels seal the tunnel opening; ore prompts, server distance enforcement, automatic out-of-range stopping, and the compact active-only HUD are implemented. Automated validation passes 11 suites and 38 tests.

Remaining playtest: re-verify spawn, unobstructed village route, every tunnel/chamber seam, collision, entrance distinction, lighting, prompt behavior, automatic stop after walking away, Mining HUD/tool-hotbar coexistence, and a small-phone viewport.
