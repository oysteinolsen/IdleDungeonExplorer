# P2.3 — Minimal Village and Mine

Status: **NEXT** — repository scene and automated checks are complete; desktop and small-phone visual playtesting remains.

**Read with:** [`P6.2-device-validation.md`](P6.2-device-validation.md) for the remaining manual check.

- Use a compact above-ground classic-fantasy village. Spawn faces an obvious objective; the mine, dungeon, forge/shop, and future leaderboard locations are only seconds apart.
- The mine and dungeon are separate, visually distinctive entrances. Both lead underground, but productive and dangerous routes must not be confused.
- The first implementation may keep village, mine, and isolated dungeon spaces in one Roblox place while services remain place-independent.
- Art direction is stylized low-poly heroic fantasy with blocky readability, warm village lighting, cooler underground lighting, recognizable Roblox avatars, and mobile-safe geometry/effects.
- The current prototype uses repository-owned primitives, built-in materials, and lights only. No third-party asset or license audit is needed for these fixtures.
- If assets are later introduced, prefer a single cohesive free family, script-free models, verified creators/CC0 sources, and an asset ledger covering source, creator, license, date, local file, Roblox ID, and modifications.

Remaining playtest: verify spawn, village-to-mine route, collision, entrance distinction, tunnel/chamber readability, lighting, Mining HUD coexistence, and small-phone presentation.
